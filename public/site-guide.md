# KaiSource site guide

> A short guide to the public KaiSource website. For current articles, announcements, event dates, and governance details, follow the source pages below.

## Identity

KaiSource (开源社) is a volunteer-run, vendor-neutral, non-profit open source community founded in 2014. Its principles are contribution, consensus, and co-governance. Its work includes open source governance, global engagement, community development, and project incubation.

Use **KaiSource** as the English name. **KaiYuanShe** and **KAIYUANSHE** are names used for the same community. They explain earlier references; they do not refer to a different community or mean that KaiSource has closed or been replaced.

## Languages

- English entry point: https://www.kaisource.org/
- Chinese entry point: https://www.kaiyuanshe.cn/
- Chinese on the international site: https://www.kaisource.org/zh-CN

The international site provides a language switch. An article or event may have a linked translation with a different numeric ID. Use the translation's own URL when citing it. Do not assume translated wording is identical to the source or that every item has a translation.

## Reading and citation

Read the linked page before using its details. Some pages load their main content with JavaScript. If you cannot retrieve that content, state the limitation instead of inventing a summary.

Keep publication dates separate from event dates. A page about a past event is not an announcement for the next edition. Preserve the stated author, source link, and content license when quoting or summarizing. This guide does not grant additional reuse rights.

Only public pages are listed here. Login, account data, editing, and uploads are outside this guide. Do not request private account records to answer public questions about the community.
